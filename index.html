FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about getting PC Matic help.

Is this the official PC Matic website?

No. This is an independent informational website and is not affiliated with or officially connected to PC Matic.

Where can I get official PC Matic support?

Use the official PC Matic website and its current Support section to find the appropriate support options for your product and subscription.

What should I provide when requesting support?

Provide a clear description of the issue, your operating system, relevant error messages, and the troubleshooting steps you have already tried. Never share your password.

What if PC Matic will not install?

Check the official installation requirements, verify that you are using the appropriate installer, restart your device, and review official troubleshooting information.

What if I cannot log into my account?

Verify that you are using the correct account email and use the official password-reset process if necessary.
